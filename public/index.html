    This pilot has no accounts, analytics, advertising, external media, microphone access, or cloud saving. It asks for no exact age or identity. All entries disappear on refresh; download portfolio evidence if you want to keep it. Your hosting provider may still keep ordinary web request logs.

    Do not enter identifying or sensitive information. Caregivers should support young learners on shared devices. Contemplative activities are optional, non-coercive, and not health advice. Disability, participation, or personal disclosure never determines a learner’s worth.

    Formal accreditation requires an appropriate institution, agreed standards, and independent assessment. This pilot does not issue credentials. Personal reflection is never evidence for spiritual attainment.
