# What is Value?

## Introduction: Why Start with Value?

In systems of governance, economics, and technology, the idea of *value* is central—but also contested. Before we tokenize anything, especially something as vital as social impact, we must ask:  
**What exactly are we valuing?**  
**How is it created, captured, and exchanged?**

---

## Value in Traditional Systems

### Fiat Economies

- Value is tied to trust in institutions (governments, banks).
- Currency derives worth from state backing, not intrinsic utility.
- Public goods (like healthcare, education) often fall outside market mechanisms—*externalities*.

### Challenges for Public Goods

- No pricing mechanism for impact.
- External value is uncaptured, leading to underfunding.
- Philanthropy or state subsidy steps in, often inefficiently.

---

## Value in Cryptonative Paradigms

### Bitcoin

- Value comes from scarcity (21 million cap) and consensus.
- Anti-inflationary, with no central authority.
- Trust is mathematical, not institutional.

### Ethereum and Beyond

- Introduces programmability of value via smart contracts.
- Enables new markets (DeFi, NFTs), but speculation dominates.
- DAOs and tokenised communities experiment with governance and impact-driven missions.

---

## Value in Digital Public Goods (DPGs)

DPGs like CARE create **social value**:

- Disease tracking  
- Health equity  
- Public data infrastructure  

**But there is no native system to measure, capture, or circulate this value.**  
This creates a paradox: high impact, low sustainability.

---

## Towards a New Value Paradigm: CIVICO’s Proposition

- Redefine value around **impact**, not speculation.
- Mint tokens *only* when real-world impact is verified.
- Enable **ethical capital** to flow into social infrastructure.
- Build governance that respects the dignity and agency of communities.

---

## Summary

- *Value* is not neutral—it reflects systems of power, trust, and design.
- The CIVICO model invites us to rethink value through the lens of **social utility**, **programmable trust**, and **inclusive governance**.
