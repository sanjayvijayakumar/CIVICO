# Why FIAT Fails DPGs

Public goods like CARE create immense social value, but fiat-based funding mechanisms often fail to sustain them due to:

- No built-in pricing or market for impact.
- Reliance on grants and subsidies.
- Misaligned incentives between funders and implementers.

This chapter explores why a new mechanism—tokenised value—is more aligned with the realities of digital public infrastructure.
